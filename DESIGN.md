## Core Principles

1. **Evidence-Driven Engineering**: Projects and code are the primary proof of capability. Factual, technical descriptions replace exaggerated marketing claims.
2. **Systems-Level Identity**: Highlighting the unique intersection of Software Engineering, Mechatronics, Robotics, and Applied AI.
3. **No Personal Photos**: Replaced with clean, abstract technical schematics, architectural typography, and live telemetry.
4. **Editorial Monochromatic Contrast**: Dominant black and warm cream with a refined 5–10% warm amber accent.
5. **High Responsiveness & Performance**: Sub-second interactions, accessible WCAG AA compliance, and clean mobile side-scrolling for secondary items.
